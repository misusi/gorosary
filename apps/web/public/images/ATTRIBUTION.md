# Artwork

The pictures in this folder are the work of other people, and they are here
because the site needed them. They are credited to the sites they came from,
and they are not ours to relicense.

## The mysteries of the Rosary — `mysteries/`

Twenty pictures, five for each of the four sets, from **The Holy Rosary**
gallery:

> <https://www.theholyrosary.org/rosary_gallery/>

- Joyful: `joyful-1.jpg` … `joyful-5.jpg` — the Annunciation, the Visitation,
  the Nativity, the Presentation in the Temple, the Finding in the Temple.
- Sorrowful: `sorrowful-1.jpg` … `sorrowful-5.jpg` — the Agony in the Garden,
  the Scourging, the Crowning with Thorns, the Carrying of the Cross, the
  Crucifixion.
- Glorious: `glorious-1.jpg` … `glorious-5.jpg` — the Resurrection, the
  Ascension, the Coming of the Holy Spirit, the Assumption, the Coronation.
- Luminous: `luminous-1.jpg` … `luminous-5.jpg` — the Baptism, the Wedding
  Feast at Cana, the Proclamation of the Kingdom, the Transfiguration, the
  Institution of the Eucharist.

## The Stations of the Cross — `stations/`

Fourteen pictures, one for each station, from **Catholic Tradition**:

> <http://www.catholictradition.org/Children/stations-cross.htm>

- `station-1.jpg` … `station-14.jpg`.

## A note on permission

Neither site states a licence for the reuse of its pictures. They are here on
the credit of the owner, and if the site is ever published commercially, or
grown much beyond what it is, they should be relicensed or replaced with
pictures that are ours to use. The file names follow the content, so
replacing them is a matter of dropping new files in with the same names.
