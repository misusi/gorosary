## How to use these reflections

Each reflection is meant to accompany one Hail Mary as you pray the decades.
Read the reflection before the decade begins, then let it settle, and let the
mystery itself do the rest. These are not explanations of the mysteries; they
are handles for the mind, so that the heart has something to hold while it
prays.

Read one set at a time. Pray the Joyful Mysteries on Mondays and Saturdays,
the Sorrowful on Tuesdays and Fridays, the Glorious on Wednesdays and Sundays,
and the Luminous on Thursdays.

## Joyful 1 — The Annunciation

Mary is doing nothing in particular when the message comes: she is at home in
Nazareth, in the ordinary house of an ordinary girl in an ordinary town. What
God asks of her will overturn the world, and He asks it of her before He asks
it of anyone else. The angel does not persuade her; he waits, and she chooses.
The first word of the New Testament is an invitation that Mary is free to
refuse. Ask her for the grace to say yes to what God asks of you, whatever it
costs.

## Joyful 2 — The Visitation

The good news does not stay in Nazareth. Mary gets up and goes, on foot, into
the hill country, to see her cousin. Elizabeth is old and childless, and the
promise given to Mary is already being fulfilled in her; two women meet in
joy, and what comes out of the meeting is the Magnificat, sung by a poor girl
in a poor village. The Church begins with a visit to someone who needs her.
Ask our Lady for the charity that carries you out of yourself and into the
house of another person today.

## Joyful 3 — The Nativity

The Son of God is born where there is no room for him. There is a stable, a
manger, and the smell of the animals, and no one to arrange anything. The
Shepherds come in from the fields because an angel told them to, and the
angels themselves cannot keep the news to themselves. God does not wait for
us to build a fitting house before he comes. Ask Him for the grace to receive
Him in the ordinary conditions of your life, in a stable if that is what you
have.

## Joyful 4 — The Presentation in the Temple

Mary brings the Child to the Temple to offer Him to the Lord, and she does so
with an old man named Simeon, who has been waiting for years to see the
Saviour. He takes the small Child in his arms and is ready to depart in
peace. Anna is there as well, and she gives thanks, and they are two old
people who have kept the promise of God alive in their own little corner. What
is given to God is not taken away from us; it is held in stronger hands. Offer
the Lord the thing you love most, and ask Him to make your heart as simple
and as ready as Simeon's.

## Joyful 5 — The Finding in the Temple

For three days Mary and Joseph look for Jesus and cannot find him, and the
text does not hide that they were afraid. They find Him in the Temple,
sitting with the teachers, and He asks them why they sought him, when they
should have known He must be in His Father's house. He was not lost; they
were the ones who had lost the way of seeing things. The same is true of us.
Ask the Lord for the grace of being found, and for the humility to be corrected
when He teaches us where He has been all along.

## Sorrowful 1 — The Agony in the Garden

Jesus goes apart to pray, and He prays in a way that is almost beyond the
human: He falls on His face, and He asks that the cup of suffering pass from
Him, and then He accepts that it will not. The disciples who should have
watched Him with Him fall asleep, and the Gospel tells us plainly that they
were heavy with sorrow. He is not asking to be spared the cross; He is asking
to be given the strength to carry it. Pray with Him in the dark hours of your
own life, and ask Him for the grace to say, "Not my will, but Thine," and to
mean it.

## Sorrowful 2 — The Scourging at the Pillar

He is stripped and bound and taken to the pillar, and the lash falls on the
back of One who has committed no sin. He is treated as the worst man in the
city before anyone has condemned Him, so that what the guilty deserve may be
laid on the innocent. The men doing it think they are indulging in a cruel
joke for an afternoon; what they are doing is the whole mystery of the
redemption. Ask the Scourged Christ for pardon for your own sins and for the
man who wronged you this week, and for the grace to forgive what has already
been forgiven for you.

## Sorrowful 3 — The Crowning with Thorns

The soldiers make a crown of thorns and press it into His head, and they put
a reed in His hand and kneel before Him in mockery. Everything they mean as
insult is the truth: this is the King of the Jews, and this is how His kingship
looks to the world. He says nothing at all, and the silence is more
terrible than any answer. Ask Him for a love that does not need to defend
itself, and ask for patience with the people who mock what they do not
understand.

## Sorrowful 4 — Jesus Carries the Cross

The condemned man is led out and made to take up the cross, and a stranger
from Cyrene is compelled to help him carry it for part of the way. The
weight is more than wood; it is everything that has ever been done against
God, gathered up and laid on the shoulders of a Galilean carpenter. Along the
road the women of Jerusalem weep, and Jesus tells them not to weep for Him.
Watch Him walk this short road. Ask Him for strength to carry the burden
that is yours, and for the grace to walk it in His company rather than alone.

## Sorrowful 5 — The Crucifixion

On Calvary the cross is raised and the sky goes dark at the middle of the day.
He is given vinegar to drink, mocked from below, and from the cross He gives
His Spirit to the Father. In the middle of all that darkness a soldier looks
up and says that this really was the Son of God, and the first Church is made
there, at the foot of a gibbet. Mary stands below with John, and the sword
that passes through her heart is the sword that passes through ours. Ask the
Crucified to forgive you your sins, and pray for those who stand beneath no
cross at all.

## Glorious 1 — The Resurrection

On the first day of the week, before anyone else has risen, Mary Magdalene is
at the empty tomb, and she is the one who runs to tell the others. They do
not believe her at first; they have to go and see for themselves. Then the
Lord appears, first to her alone and then to His frightened friends, and He
shows them the wounds He carries and wishes them peace. The stone was rolled
back because death could not keep Him. Ask the risen Christ to raise your
hopes where they have fallen, and to bring you the joy of this day.

## Glorious 2 — The Ascension

The Lord stays with His disciples for forty days and then is lifted up before
their eyes and taken into heaven. They stand there looking up, and two men in
white tell them to stop looking and to get on with the work. He leaves them,
but everything He has promised is left behind with them: His Spirit, His
word, His Church, and His word that He will come again. The way upward is not
an escape from the world but an entrance into a fuller life, and He shares
that life with us by grace. Ask Jesus to keep us faithful while He is away,
and to lift our hearts to where He has gone.

## Glorious 3 — The Coming of the Holy Spirit

The disciples are gathered in one place, and the Holy Spirit comes upon them
like a wind, and tongues of fire rest on them and they are filled with it.
Peter, who denied Christ three times, stands up and proclaims Him. The same
Spirit who rested on the Child in the cave at Bethlehem is now speaking in the
marketplace through fishermen. The Church was born in an upper room and she
was born to speak. Ask the Holy Spirit to fill your work, your home, and your
conversation, and to make you a willing instrument in the building up of
Christ's body.

## Glorious 4 — The Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary

The Apostles gather around Mary's tomb, and the Lord Himself comes for her and
takes her, body and soul, into the glory He shares with the Saints. The
Church has always believed that she stands with her Son in a glory beyond our
own. The mystery rests on the same promise as the Resurrection: the body is
not left to the corruption of the grave, and death has no final hold on those
whom God has taken. Mary is assumed because her whole life was for this. Ask
her for a heart like hers, free of what is unnecessary and given wholly to
God, and ask her to bring us to the resurrection she believes in.

## Glorious 5 — The Coronation of the Blessed Virgin Mary

The last of the Glorious Mysteries is the crowning of Mary as Queen of
Heaven and of earth, a scene the Church has painted and sung for centuries
though it stands outside the pages of Scripture. The crown is given to the
woman whose whole life was the words, "Behold the handmaid of the Lord."
Everything she was, the Annunciation, the Magnificat, the Child in the
manger, the shadow of the cross beneath which she stood, is gathered into this
one moment of honour. And the honour is not hers alone; it is God's own glory
coming back to the humblest creature He ever made. Ask Our Lady to intercede
for us before her Son, and ask her to teach us the humility that makes such a
crown possible.

## Luminous 1 — The Baptism of the Lord

John the Baptist sees Jesus coming out of the Jordan and asks whether He
should come to Him, and Jesus answers that it is fitting for them to fulfil
all justice, and then He is baptized in the water. The heavens open, the
Spirit comes down like a dove, and the Father's voice is heard saying that
this is the beloved Son. The Son of God takes the place of sinners at the
very river where they were washed. Ask the Holy Spirit to renew in you what
was begun at your own Baptism, and to make you a clean dwelling place for
God.

## Luminous 2 — The Wedding Feast at Cana

Mary is at a wedding, and when the wine runs out she tells Jesus and says
only that they have no wine. Six stone jars are filled with water and drawn
out as wine, and the first public sign of Our Lord's power is worked in an
ordinary house, at the request of His mother. She keeps the beginning of the
mystery to herself and sends the servants to the Bridegroom. God does not
usually announce where He is going to work. Ask our Lady for the hiddenness
of this first miracle, and ask Jesus for joy, and for marriage rightly
ordered and rightly kept.

## Luminous 3 — The Proclamation of the Kingdom of God

Leaving the desert, where the devil had offered Him all the kingdoms of the
world for nothing, Jesus begins to preach in the towns of Galilee: the
kingdom of heaven is at hand, repent, and believe the Gospel. He calls
disciples away from their work, and the good news travels from village to
village and then to the ends of the earth. The kingdom He announces is not a
programme but a life to be entered upon, and it begins with a single word,
repent. Ask Jesus to forgive us our sins, to turn our lives around, and to
give us courage to proclaim what He has proclaimed.

## Luminous 4 — The Transfiguration

Jesus takes three of His closest friends up a mountain and is transfigured
before them, His face shining as the sun and His garments white as light, and
the Father's voice says, This is My beloved Son; hear Him. The disciples are
overcome, and Peter offers to build three shelters for a Lord who is not
going to stay in any of them, and Jesus tells them to get down to the people.
The mountain is not the end of the story; the mountain is the light that
shines down from it. Ask the risen Christ to let some of His glory rest on
us now, and to give us strength to come down and serve.

## Luminous 5 — The Institution of the Eucharist

On the eve of His Passion Jesus takes bread and wine, gives thanks over them,
and says that this is His body and this His blood, the new covenant shed for
many for the forgiveness of sins. He who has just washed the disciples' feet
now feeds them, and He who is about to be betrayed gives Himself to be eaten
and drunk. What He did on that table He has left to be done in every age, and
He has not left it to be forgotten. This is the hidden heart of all the
mysteries, because the Passion is given to us under the appearance of bread
and wine. Ask Jesus to make us worthy to receive Him, and to love Him in our
neighbour as He has loved us.
