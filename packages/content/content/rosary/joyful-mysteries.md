      - n: 1
        title: The Annunciation
        scripture: Luke 1:26-38
        intention: For the grace of a humble heart that is ready to be used by God.
        meditation: >
          The angel Gabriel is sent by God to announce to Mary that she will conceive
          and bear the Son of God. He greets her with the words "Hail, full of grace,
          the Lord is with thee." Mary is troubled, and she asks how this can be,
          since she knows no man. The answer is the Holy Spirit: "The Holy Spirit
          shall come upon thee, and the power of the Most High shall overshadow thee."
          And Mary says the words that have echoed through every century of the
          Church: "Behold, I am the handmaid of the Lord; be it done according to thy
          word." We ask her for the same spirit of obedience — not hesitation, but
          trust that goes ahead of understanding.
      - n: 2
        title: The Visitation
        scripture: Luke 1:39-56
        intention: For charity and for those who are visited in their need.
        meditation: >
          Mary rises up quickly and goes into the hill country of Judea to visit her
          cousin Elizabeth. When she arrives, Elizabeth is filled with the Holy
          Spirit and cries out: "Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the
          fruit of thy womb." Mary cannot contain herself and sings the Magnificat,
          the song that has been sung in churches for two thousand years: "My soul
          doth magnify the Lord, and my spirit rejoices in God my Saviour." Two
          women, both with child, and between them the saving of the world. This
          mystery teaches us that the mission of the Church begins in a visit, in
          the ordinary act of going to someone who needs us.
      - n: 3
        title: The Nativity
        scripture: Luke 2:1-24
        intention: For the love of God in the humility of the Child of Bethlehem.
        meditation: >
          Caesar issues a decree that all the world be enrolled, and Joseph and Mary
          must go to Bethlehem, to the city of David. There, with no room in the
          inn, they find only a stable. And there she bears the Son of God. She
          lays Him in a manger, because there was no bed. The shepherds arrive,
          instructed by an angel, and the angelic host breaks into song: "Glory to
          God in the highest, and on earth peace to men of good will." The mystery
          teaches us that God does not skip the ordinary. He comes in the middle of
          human history, in a stable, in a family like ours.
      - n: 4
        title: The Presentation in the Temple
        scripture: Luke 2:25-38
        intention: For the dedication of ourselves to the Lord.
        meditation: >
          When the days of her purification were fulfilled, Mary and Joseph bring
          the Child to the temple in Jerusalem to present Him to the Lord. There
          Simeon, a righteous and faithful man, takes Him in his arms and says: "Now
          lettest thou thy servant depart in peace, according to thy word; for mine
          eyes have seen thy salvation." Anna the prophetess also comes, and gives
          thanks to God. The old Simeon holds the small Child and recognizes the
          Savior. The mystery teaches us that what is given to God is never taken
          away from us — it is held in stronger hands.
      - n: 5
        title: The Finding in the Temple
        scripture: Luke 2:49-52
        intention: For the love of the house of God and for those who are lost.
        meditation: >
          After the feasts of Passover, Mary and Joseph return to Jerusalem and
          cannot find the Child. Three days of searching, heart heavy, until they
          find Him in the temple, sitting among the teachers, listening and asking
          them questions. Mary says: "Why have you done this to us? Behold your
          father and I have been seeking you with sorrow." And Jesus answers: "How
          is it that you sought me? Did you not know that I must be in my Father's
          house?" The mystery teaches us two things at once: that it is right to
          search for God, and that we are often surprised to find Him nearer than
          we thought.

## How to pray this set

Pray the Sign of the Cross. Then say one **Our Father**, three **Hail Marys**, one
**Glory Be**. For each of the five decades, pray one Hail Mary while you contemplate
the mystery, and one Hail Mary for each of the ten Hail Marys that follow.

After each decade, say the **Glory Be** and a short **Hail Mary** with the
intention of the mystery you are praying. After the fifth decade, say the
**Hail, Holy Queen**, and finish with a final **Hail Mary** and **Glory Be**.
