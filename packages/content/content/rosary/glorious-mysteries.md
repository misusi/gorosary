## The five mysteries

The Glorious Mysteries are the mystery of triumph. They begin in an empty tomb
and end in a throne, and they are prayed on Wednesdays and Sundays and
throughout the Easter season.

1. **The Resurrection** — the Lord alive on the first day of the week, and the
   empty tomb.
2. **The Ascension** — lifted up into heaven, with the promise of His return.
3. **The Coming of the Holy Spirit** — the wind and the fire, and the Church
   speaking.
4. **The Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary** — taken to the glory of her
   Son.
5. **The Coronation of the Blessed Virgin Mary** — the crown given to the
   handmaid of the Lord.

## How to pray this set

Pray the Sign of the Cross. Then say one **Our Father**, three **Hail Marys**,
one **Glory Be**. For each of the five decades, pray one Hail Mary while you
contemplate the mystery, and one Hail Mary for each of the ten Hail Marys that
follow.

After each decade, say the **Glory Be** and a short **Hail Mary** with the
intention of the mystery you are praying. After the fifth decade, say the
**Hail, Holy Queen**, and finish with a final **Hail Mary** and **Glory Be**.

Pray these with thanksgiving rather than grief. It is a good habit to pray
them before Mass on Sunday, and to keep a light burning while you do, if you
can. The resurrection of Christ is the ground of every other hope we have.
