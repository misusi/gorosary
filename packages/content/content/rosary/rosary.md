## What the Rosary is

The Rosary is a Scripture-based meditation. It is not a string of repetitions to be
rushed through; it is a structured way of walking through the life of Jesus Christ
with Mary as the one who shows us the way to Him.

At its centre are **five decades** of ten Hail Marys each — fifty Hail Marys in all
— one after the other, with a meditation on a different mystery between each
decade. Around them go the **Our Father**, said once at the beginning of each
decade, the **Glory Be**, said at the end of each decade, and the **Hail, Holy
Queen** at the end of the Rosary.

## Why fifty-four Hail Marys

There are fifty Hail Marys in the decades, plus three after the Glory Be at the
start (the "three Hail Marys of the Rosary"), plus one after the Hail, Holy Queen
at the end. Fifty-four in total, echoing the fifty-four Hail Marys traditionally
said in a day — one for each year of the life of the Blessed Virgin Mary.

## The four sets of mysteries

The mysteries are grouped into four sets of five, and the set prayed today is
determined by the liturgical calendar.

- The **Joyful Mysteries** — the Annunciation, the Visitation, the Nativity, the
  Presentation, and the Finding in the Temple. Prayed on Mondays and Saturdays, and
  throughout Advent, Christmas, and Ordinary Time.
- The **Sorrowful Mysteries** — the Agony in the Garden, the Scourging, the
  Crowning with Thorns, the Carrying of the Cross, and the Crucifixion. Prayed on
  Tuesdays and Fridays, and throughout Lent.
- The **Glorious Mysteries** — the Resurrection, the Ascension, the Coming of the
  Holy Spirit, the Assumption, and the Coronation of the Blessed Virgin Mary.
  Prayed on Wednesdays and Sundays, and throughout the Easter season.
- The **Luminous Mysteries** — the Baptism, the Wedding Feast at Cana, the
  Proclamation of the Kingdom, the Transfiguration, and the Institution of the
  Eucharist. Traditionally prayed on Thursdays, and at the Angelus.

## The rosary in short

1. Make the Sign of the Cross.
2. Say the **Act of Contrition** if you are going to confession, or the short
   act of contrition.
3. Say one **Our Father**.
4. Say three **Hail Marys**.
5. Say one **Glory Be**.
6. For each of the five decades: say one **Our Father**, ten **Hail Marys** with
   a meditation between each decade, then one **Glory Be**.
7. Say the **Hail, Holy Queen**.
8. Say one final **Hail Mary**, one **Glory Be**, and a short prayer.

## A note on attention

Praying the Rosary well is not a matter of finishing quickly. A decade prayed in
five minutes with the mind on the phone is worth less than one decade prayed
slowly with the mystery actually in front of you. If you miss a mystery, do not
stop — pick it up the next time. The Rosary is a lifetime of prayer, not a
performance.
