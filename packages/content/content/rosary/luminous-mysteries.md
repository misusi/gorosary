## The five mysteries

The Luminous Mysteries were added to the Rosary in the sixteenth century, and
they are the mysteries of the public ministry of Jesus Christ. They are prayed
on Thursdays, and they are the choice of many for the Angelus at dawn, at noon,
and at dusk.

1. **The Baptism of the Lord** — the heavens opened, and the Spirit came down
   like a dove.
2. **The Wedding Feast at Cana** — water turned into wine at the request of
   His mother.
3. **The Proclamation of the Kingdom of God** — repent, for the kingdom of
   heaven is at hand.
4. **The Transfiguration** — the light on the mountain and the voice of the
   Father.
5. **The Institution of the Eucharist** — the body and the blood given under
   the appearances of bread and wine.

## How to pray this set

Pray the Sign of the Cross. Then say one **Our Father**, three **Hail Marys**,
one **Glory Be**. For each of the five decades, pray one Hail Mary while you
contemplate the mystery, and one Hail Mary for each of the ten Hail Marys that
follow.

Each mystery is announced before its decade, and its intention is prayed with the
tenth **Hail Mary**. After each decade, say the **Glory Be** and the **Oh My
Jesus**. After the fifth decade, say the **Hail, Holy Queen**, and close with a
short prayer.

These five mysteries are gathered around the One who reveals Himself publicly.
Pray them with attention, and do not let the fifth of them pass without a
serious petition for the grace of a clean and fruitful Communion.
