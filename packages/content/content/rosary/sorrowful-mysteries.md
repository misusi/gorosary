## The five mysteries

The Sorrowful Mysteries walk through the last day of the life of Jesus Christ,
from the garden in which He prayed to the cross on which He died. They are
prayed on Tuesdays and Fridays, and throughout Lent, and they are prayed
slowly.

1. **The Agony in the Garden** — Jesus in Gethsemane, asking His Father that
   the cup may pass, and then accepting it.
2. **The Scourging at the Pillar** — the innocent condemned in the place of the
   guilty.
3. **The Crowning with Thorns** — the King of the Jews mocked as a king.
4. **Jesus Carries the Cross** — the road from the judgment hall to Calvary.
5. **The Crucifixion** — the death on the cross, and the sign of it in the
   centurion's confession of faith.

## How to pray this set

Pray the Sign of the Cross. Then say one **Our Father**, three **Hail Marys**,
one **Glory Be**. For each of the five decades, pray one Hail Mary while you
contemplate the mystery, and one Hail Mary for each of the ten Hail Marys that
follow.

Each mystery is announced before its decade, and its intention is prayed with the
tenth **Hail Mary**. After each decade, say the **Glory Be** and the **Oh My
Jesus**. After the fifth decade, say the **Hail, Holy Queen**, and close with a
short prayer.

The Sorrowful Mysteries are best prayed quietly and without hurry. If you can
do so, pray them before the altar of a church, or in a quiet room, and do not
let the mind wander from the mystery without gently bringing it back.
