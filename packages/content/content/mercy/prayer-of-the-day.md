    title: Day 1 — For the Apostles
    text: >
      Say the Prayer of the Day: O merciful Jesus, whose sight is the fountain
      of all goodness and the inexhaustible treasure of all grace and mercy,
      grant us, we beseech You, that we may behold in You our God as He is. We
      beseech You, by the Blood of Your precious Heart and by Your wounds, to
      heal the wounds of our hearts, and to pour out upon the world the mercy
      You have kept hidden in Your divine Heart. Grant us, O merciful Jesus, to
      see Your mercy and Your infinite power in the world, and to be worthy of
      the grace of being carriers of Your mercy, and of announcing Your
      goodness to the world. Let Your mercy, O Jesus, be not a mere word of
      comfort to us, but an active force in our souls, and in the whole world.
      May the omnipotence of Your mercy, O Jesus Christ, be displayed on the
      earth as it is in heaven. We beseech You, O Lord Jesus, for Your
      Apostles and for those who in our own day are sent to preach Your
      Gospel: fill them with Your Holy Spirit, that they may speak Your word
      with the boldness of the Apostles, and make them instruments of the
      mercy of Your Sacred Heart. Amen. Then say the Hail Mary: Hail Mary, full
      of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and
      blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray
      for us sinners, now, and at the hour of our death. Amen. Then say: O
      Holy Blood, why do you not flow? Why do you not run like a river, from
      the One who is the Font of Life? O Blood, worthily does Your worthiness
      pour forth the blood of the One who is Father, Son and Holy Spirit, with
      such power that it did not waver at the moment of the Passion, when the
      whole world was ransomed. O Blood, what have You not done for me? How
      have I not been able to thank You, O Blood, for the new and the great
      miracle of the mercy of God, which You brought to light in the world? O
      Blood, when I fall asleep, I shall see the world flooded with Your
      Blood, and I shall be saved. And when I am in the world of the damned, I
      shall beg You, O Blood, that You flood my heart with Your love and that I
      sing a song of thanksgiving to You: O Blood, I love You, and You are my
      hope. O Blood, when I am in the world of the damned, I beg You that the
      taste of Your love be the delight of my heart. O Blood, if I cannot love
      You, at least let me be always mindful of You and never forget You. O
      Blood, grant that I may love You with all my heart, with all my soul,
      and with all my strength. Then say: O my Father, King of Heaven and
      Earth, who disposes of all things as You please, grant me the grace to
      embrace my crosses, to merit an aggravation of my pain for the sake of
      Your greater glory, and to make these crosses a source of great merit
      for souls, for the sake of Your merciful love and the edification of
      Your Church, in which You desire to be praised for ever and ever. Amen.

## The Nine Days

The **Feast of the Divine Mercy** falls on the Sunday after the Second Sunday
of Easter. The nine days before it are set aside for the **Prayer of the
Day**, said once each day, so that the mercy of Christ may be asked for the
whole world before the feast is kept.

The nine days are said in order. **Day 1** is for the Apostles; **Day 2** is
for the Apostles and their successors, that is, for the Church Militant; **Day
3** is for the conversion of sinners and for lukewarm Christians; **Day 4** is
for heretics and schismatics; **Day 5** is for the conversion of the Jews and
the Moslems; **Day 6** is for the Holy Souls in Purgatory; **Day 7** is for
the help of the Christian and the grace of confession; **Day 8** is for the
conversion of the fallen; and **Day 9** is for the Church, for the Holy
Father, and for all Christian people.

Each day's prayer is said once, and it is said in full. The **Chaplet of
Divine Mercy** is said before it, at the hour of eleven, as it is said every
day of the year.

## The Eleventh of the Month

The **Prayer of the Day** is also said on its own on the **eleventh day of
every month**, the day on which Our Lady of the Gate of Dawn appeared at
Kraków. On that day the Church asks Our Lord for the grace of seeing clearly
that His mercy is greater than His justice, and that His mercy reaches every
creature, without exception and without measure.

## The Prayer of the Day

> O merciful Jesus, whose sight is the fountain of all goodness and the
> inexhaustible treasure of all grace and mercy, grant us, we beseech You,
> that we may behold in You our God as He is. We beseech You, by the Blood of
> Your precious Heart and by Your wounds, to heal the wounds of our hearts,
> and to pour out upon the world the mercy You have kept hidden in Your divine
> Heart. Grant us, O merciful Jesus, to see Your mercy and Your infinite power
> in the world, and to be worthy of the grace of being carriers of Your mercy,
> and of announcing Your goodness to the world. Let Your mercy, O Jesus, be not
> a mere word of comfort to us, but an active force in our souls, and in the
> whole world. May the omnipotence of Your mercy, O Jesus Christ, be displayed
> on the earth as it is in heaven. We beseech You, O Jesus, by the Blood of
> Your precious Heart and by Your wounds, to be for us a living fountain of
> mercy, and to pour out upon the world the mercy You have kept hidden in Your
> divine Heart. Amen.

## The Prayer to St. Michael

> By the intercession of St. Michael the Archangel and of all the saints, O
> Lord, grant us the grace to be set free from sin and to escape the danger of
> eternal death. And vouchsafe, we beseech You, O Lord, that we may be
> supported by the intercession of the Blessed Virgin Mary, of St. Michael the
> Archangel, and of all the saints. Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

## The Three Flowers of the Day

The Chaplet is traditionally offered for three intentions of the day, which
are called the three flowers of that day. Any three may be chosen, and no two
days need have the same:

- The **intention of the day** — the work God has given you, the people in your house, and those whose names you carry in your heart.
- The **intention of the Church** — for the Holy Father, for bishops and priests, for the faithful, and for the missions.
- The **intention of the Holy Souls** — for those who are in Purgatory and have no one to pray for them.

## A Note

The nine days are said quietly, and they are said as they are written. Nothing
is gained by hurrying through the prayer of the day in order to get to the
Chaplet, and nothing is gained by adding to it. The mercy of Christ is asked
for with the whole heart, and the whole heart is what is asked for.
