## Before You Begin

### The Picture of the Divine Mercy

In the picture Our Lord stands with His right hand raised in blessing, and His
left hand touching His chest. From His heart there issue two rays of light:
one pale, falling upon the green land of Poland, and one bright and red,
shining over the sea and over the rest of the world. The rays are blood and
water. The whole of the picture is bathed in gold, and beneath it Our Lord says
the words that are the whole of the message: *Jesus, I trust in You.*

The blood and the water are not decoration. They are what flows from the
heart of the risen Christ, and they are the mercy that is wanted — the mercy
that forgives sins, that heals the soul, and that is not given according to
merit.

### The Eleven O'Clock Prayer

The Chaplet is said at **eleven o'clock in the morning**, at the hour of Our
Lady of the Gate of Dawn, who appeared at Kraków in the year 1931. Eleven is
the hour of mercy, and the Chaplet belongs to it. Whoever says it at that
hour is taken up, as it were, into the prayer of the Church, and joins the
company of all those who have said it at the same hour, in every part of the
world.

### The Five Decades

The Chaplet is a rosary of five decades, and each decade has its own
intention:

- The **First Decade**, for the Lord's Prayer, that is, for the spreading of the Gospel and for those who preach it.
- The **Second Decade**, for the Hail Mary, for the love of the Blessed Virgin Mary and for the multiplication of her children.
- The **Third Decade**, for the Church Militant — for the Holy Father, for bishops, for priests and religious, and for the whole Church at work in the world.
- The **Fourth Decade**, for the Holy Souls in Purgatory, who cannot pray for themselves.
- The **Fifth Decade**, for the Holy Church, for its holiness and its unity, and for the spread of the mercy of Christ through it.

### The Three Flowers of the Day

The Chaplet is traditionally offered for three intentions of the day, and the
three are called the three flowers of that day. Any three may be chosen, and
no two days need have the same:

- The **intention of the day** — the work God has given you, the people in your house, and those whose names you carry in your heart.
- The **intention of the Church** — for the Holy Father, for bishops and priests, for the faithful, and for the missions.
- The **intention of the Holy Souls** — for those who are in Purgatory and have no one to pray for them.

The general intention of the whole Chaplet is always the same: the conversion
of sinners, and the mercy of Jesus Christ.

## The Prayer of the Day

> O merciful Jesus, whose sight is the fountain of all goodness and the
> inexhaustible treasure of all grace and mercy, grant us, we beseech You,
> that we may behold in You our God as He is. We beseech You, by the Blood of
> Your precious Heart and by Your wounds, to heal the wounds of our hearts,
> and to pour out upon the world the mercy You have kept hidden in Your divine

> in the world, and to be worthy of the grace of being carriers of Your mercy,
> and of announcing Your goodness to the world. Let Your mercy, O Jesus, be not
> a mere word of comfort to us, but an active force in our souls, and in the

> divine Heart. Amen.

## The Prayer to St. Michael

> By the intercession of St. Michael the Archangel and of all the saints, O
> Lord, grant us the grace to be set free from sin and to escape the danger of
> eternal death. And vouchsafe, we beseech You, O Lord, that we may be
> supported by the intercession of the Blessed Virgin Mary, of St. Michael the
> Archangel, and of all the saints. Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

## How to Pray It

1. Make the **Sign of the Cross**.
2. Say one **Our Father**.
3. Say **three Hail Marys**. The traditional explanation given for them is that they are for the three theological virtues — faith, hope, and charity.
4. Say one **Glory Be**.
5. For each of the **five decades**: say the opening prayer of the decade (*Eternal Father, through Your mercy and compassion...*), then the **Our Father**, then on each of the ten beads the prayer *O my Father, King of Heaven and Earth...*, each bead with a particular intention.
6. At the end of a decade, say the prayer *Holy Father, Holy Spirit, Holy God...*, and then one **Hail Mary**. In the fuller traditional form the prayer *O Holy Blood...* is said after each decade as well.
7. After the fifth decade, say the **Prayer of the Day** and the **Prayer to St. Michael**.

The **Prayer of the Day** may also be said on its own on the eleventh day of
each month, and the **Chaplet** is said on each of the nine days before the
Feast of the Divine Mercy.
