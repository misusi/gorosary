## The Saint

Francis was the son of a merchant of Assisi, and for some years he lived the
life of a rich young man of his city. Then he went out to pray in the
countryside, and heard a voice from a crucifix telling him to build the house of
the Lord, not with money but with himself. He gave back his clothes to the poor
man who mocked him for them, and that was the beginning.

He gathered a few companions, who became the Friars Minor. He preached to the
birds and to the wolves. He brought a leper into his arms. He stood before the
Emperor and before the Sultan and told them the truth about their souls. And on
the third of October, the day of his death, the wounds of Christ were given to
his hands and feet and side, and he would tell no one until nearly the end.

He is the patron of the poor, of animals, of the birds, of peace, of poverty,
and of the environment. He is called the seraphic saint, and his tomb at Assisi
is a place of pilgrimage. His feast is kept on the fourth of October.

## The Nine Days

These nine prayers begin with the renunciation of the first day and end with the
stigmata and the death, which are the same day in his life. The middle days
reach out to the poor, to the animals, to the enemies of peace, and to Saint
Clare, who walked the same road with him.

The novena is suited to a family. Say it at the end of the day, when the
household is gathered, and let the fifth day be said especially for the
creatures of God and for those who have no voice.

## How to Pray It

Make the Sign of the Cross, then say the prayer of the day, then the Rosary of
five decades, with one more decade for the intention of the novena. On the
first and ninth days, fast from bread and water until noon and give alms to the
poor in honour of the saint who gave everything away.

St. Francis is the patron of those who have nothing, and he is also the patron
of those who have too much. Bring him your restlessness, your anger, your
discomfort, and your attachment to what you own. He is very good at loosening
knots, and he does it without making a fuss about it.
