## The Saint

Anthony was born in Lisbon of a noble family, and studied there and at
Bologna, and for a time he taught the letters of the saints in the great
university. Then he gave it all up and joined the Friars Minor in a poor little
friary in distant Portugal. He asked for the poorest house in the whole
province, and he was sent to a place called Rimini, and to the little town of
Pao where the people were thick and blind.

Then he began to preach, and the Church has never quite got over it. At Lisbon
the crowd was so great that the square was full and the roofs were full, and
the fish of the sea came up and held its head above the water to listen to him.
The Church has called him a Doctor, though he would have called himself
nothing but a poor friar with a Child in his arms.

He is the patron of the return of what is lost: lost objects, lost money, and
above all lost souls. He is also the patron of preachers and of the poor. His
feast is kept on the thirteenth of June.

## The Nine Days

The nine prayers below begin with the lost thing and end with the Doctor of the
Church. Read them one a day, and name the loss out loud before you begin: the
object, the relationship, the job, the way of living, the soul.

St. Anthony is a friend of beginners. Many people who would be helped by
praying to him are shy about praying to the great saints at all. He is the
saint for the shy.

## How to Pray It

Make the Sign of the Cross, say the prayer of the day, then the Rosary: five
decades, with one more decade for the intention of the novena. On the first and
ninth days, fast from bread and water until noon and give alms, in the name of
St. Anthony, to someone who has lost something.

His feast is in June, but the novena may be made at any time, and it is
customarily made when something has actually been lost, or when a person has
strayed away from God and someone cannot stop worrying about them.
