## Our Lady of the Rosary

The Rosary is the summary of the Gospel and the summary of the life of Jesus
Christ, and it was given to the Church to be said with Mary in the middle of
it. St. Dominic is traditionally credited with receiving the devotion in the
form we know, in answer to the Albigensian heresy, and the tradition of the
battle of Lepanto in 1571 gave the Rosary its title of the victory of the
faithful.

She is called Star of the Sea in the Litany of Loreto, and Mother of the Rosary
by the Church, and Queen of the Rosary by the faithful. Her feast is kept on the
seventh of October, the anniversary of Lepanto, and on the day of the Annunciation.

The Rosary is the one devotion that has never needed explaining to anybody. A
child can make it. A person with no education can make it. A person with no
strength left can make it, because the Our Father and the Hail Mary are
carried in the memory, and Mary is holding the other end of it.

## The Nine Days

The nine prayers below begin with Mary as Star and end with the Church. They are
written to be said aloud, and they are the traditional prayers that have
accompanied the Rosary for centuries.

The Rosary is the substance of this novena. When the prayers are said, the
Rosary itself is the point: it is the shape of the life of Christ given to you
by his mother, and it is the thing that will be left with you when the novena
is over.

## How to Pray It

Make the Sign of the Cross, say the prayer of the day, and then say the
Rosary with the mystery of the day of the Rosary in mind for each decade. Add
one more decade for the intention of the novena. On the first and ninth days,
fast from bread and water until noon and give alms, so that the Rosary may
spread among those who have none.

Say this novena in October if you can, and say the Rosary with it. The old
custom was that the Rosary was said in company, one voice leading and the
others answering, and a Rosary said alone is better than a Rosary not said at
all.
