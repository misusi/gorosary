## The Heart of Jesus

The Sacred Heart of Jesus is the love of God made visible. John tells us that
the soldier opened the side of Jesus with a lance, and that from the wound came
out blood and water, and that the Church has ever read in that wound the
opened door of the Heart of Jesus. The Sacred Heart is not a feeling we have
about Jesus; it is the way God has chosen to love us, given His Son to be
wounded for us and to rise again.

The Church has called this heart the heart of the family, because the family of
God is the family of the Heart of Jesus: the Father, the Son, and the Holy
Spirit, and in Him the men and women who keep his commandments. To live in that
family is the whole of the Christian vocation. Reparation, the offering of
ourselves for the sins of others, belongs to that family too, since it was the
Heart of Jesus that was wounded out of love for us.

The Solemnity of the Sacred Heart of Jesus is kept on the Friday after Corpus
Christi.

## The Nine Days

The nine prayers below are ordered after the Heart itself: its love, its
family, its wounds, its reparation, its mercy, its kingdom, its shepherds, our
own homes, and finally the surrender of the heart itself. They are best said
before the Blessed Sacrament, or at least in the presence of a crucifix.

Make this novena for a particular person as well as for yourself. Most of the
family of the Heart of Jesus are in the world and do not yet know they belong
to it, and they are far more likely to come home through the love of a
particular friend than through an argument.

## How to Pray It

Make the Sign of the Cross, say the prayer of the day, then the Rosary of five
decades, and one more decade for the intention of the novena. On the first and
ninth days, go to Mass if you can, and receive Holy Communion; fast from bread
and water until noon, and give alms to the poor, who are the ones the Sacred
Heart loves without measure.

Where it is possible, the nine days should be joined to the nine days of the
Solemnity, and the novena should be said in a church. Where that is not
possible, say it at home, before a crucifix, and do not hurry it.
