## The Morning Prayer

Say it slowly, before the day's work begins. It takes about two minutes, and it
is worth more than the two minutes cost.

Make the **Sign of the Cross**:

> In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

## A Psalm of the Morning

Sing or say the opening of Psalm 95, the psalm of the morning:

> Come, let us praise the Lord: let us heartily rejoice in the strength of our
> salvation. Let us come before His presence with thanksgiving, and show
> ourselves glad in Him with psalms. (Psalm 95:1-2)

> Alleluia.

## The Invocation of the Holy Trinity

> Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, as it was in
> the beginning, is now, and will be forever. Amen.

> O God, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, three Persons in one God, who lives and
> reigns forever: I praise You and I bless You. Be with me this day, and keep me
> in Your grace. Let all that I do this day be Yours, and let me begin it with
> You and end it with You.

## The Petitions

Offer the day to God, one petition at a time:

- Lord Jesus Christ, teach me to do Your will today, and to please You in everything I do.
- Blessed Virgin Mary, Mother of God, pray for me and for those I love.
- St. Joseph, and all the saints of God, intercede for me.
- My guardian angel, stand by me today, and keep me from all evil.
- For the Church of Christ, for the Pope, and for all the faithful.
- For my family, for my work, and for those who have gone far from God.

## The Offering

> O Lord, I offer You this day: my work, my family, my health, and my heart.
> Take them all, and do with them what is best for me and for Your glory.

## The Blessing

> God of all good gifts, who has never left me without help, bless this day and
> all that I shall do in it. Let me begin with You, go through it with You, and
> end it with You; and when it is ended, let me rest in Your peace. Through
> Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

## When to Say It

The traditional time is on rising, before breakfast and before the day's
occupations. It is said again, in the same form, if a person is beginning some
important undertaking, and many say the **Prayer for the Grace of the Holy
Spirit** before each task of the day.

At the close of the day, the **Evening Prayer** takes its place.
