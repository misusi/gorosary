## The Traditional Form

This is said after the examination of conscience in the **Evening Prayer**, and
it is the prayer to say when one is about to go to confession.

> O my Lord and my God, I am sorry that I have offended You, because I know
> that You are good and that You alone are worthy of all my love. I am sorry
> because I dread the loss of Heaven and the pains of Hell, but most of all
> because I have offended You, my good and sovereign Lord. I firmly resolve,
> with the help of Your grace, to amend my ways and to sin no more. I firmly
> resolve never again to offend You, and to fly from all occasions of sin. I
> firmly resolve to conceal nothing from You, and to confess all my sins
> humbly. In the name of Jesus Christ my Lord. Amen.

A longer traditional form is also said in some households and religious
communities:

> I, my Lord and my God, have sinned against You, in the sight of the angels
> and in the sight of all Heaven, and in the sight of Your holy Church; for I
> have sinned against You, I have sinned against You, I have sinned against
> You. My Lord, because I want to do so, and because of my sins. I firmly
> resolve, with the help of Your grace, to do penance and to amend my life, and
> to sin no more. My Lord, I firmly resolve never again to offend You, and to
> fly from all occasions of sin. I firmly resolve to conceal nothing from You,
> and to confess all my sins humbly. In the name of Jesus Christ my Lord. Amen.

## The Short Form

> Jesus, my Lord and my God, I am sorry that I have offended You. I am sorry
> because I dread the loss of Heaven and the pains of Hell, but most of all
> because I have offended You, my good and sovereign Lord. I firmly resolve,
> with Your grace, to amend my ways and to sin no more. Amen.

## A Note

An act of contrition is not a feeling. It is an act: a turning back. The words
matter less than the turning, and the Church asks only that the sorrow be
genuine and that the resolution be firm, and that the person do what the
resolution promises — go to confession, make restitution, and put the fallen
thing right.
