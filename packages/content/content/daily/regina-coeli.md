## The Prayer

It is said kneeling, or standing with a bowed head, in place of the Angelus:

> Queen of Heaven, rejoice. Alleluia. For the Lord is risen, indeed, and hath
> shone upon us. Alleluia. Pray for us to the Lord. O Virgin Mary. Amen.

The fuller traditional English form runs:

> Queen of Heaven, rejoice! Alleluia: for the Lord, our God, is risen, and
> hath shone upon us. Alleluia. Pray for us to the Lord, O Virgin Mary: that we
> may be made worthy to be blessed with Thee in the kingdom of heaven. Amen.

## The Prayer in Latin

> Regina caeli, laetare. Alleluia. Quia surrexit Dominus vere, sicut dixit tibi.
> Alleluia. Ora pro nobis, Virgo Maria. Amen.

## When It Is Said

The Regina Coeli is said **from Easter until Pentecost**, at the same three
hours as the Angelus — dawn, noon, and dusk — and it takes the place of the
Angelus for the whole of the Paschal season. The bells of the parish are rung
as they are for the Angelus.

The change is not arbitrary. During Lent, when the Church walks with Christ
toward the Cross, the Ave Maria of the Annunciation is the fitting prayer, with
its "Behold the handmaid of the Lord." At Easter, the Angel speaks again, and
the word of the Lord is, *He is risen*. The prayer of the Church turns with it,
from theAnnunciation to the empty tomb, and the Alleluia is let loose.

## The Short Doxology

The Regina Coeli is commonly followed by the **Glory Be**:

> Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, as it was in
> the beginning, is now, and will be forever. Amen.

Some say the **Hail Mary** as well, since the Regina Coeli is a Marian prayer
and does not, in its own right, contain the Ave Maria.

## A Note on the Name

The name comes from the first words, *Regina caeli, laetare* — Queen of
Heaven, rejoice. It is one of the oldest Marian antiphons of the Church, and
it is one of the very few that are still sung with the same words in Latin
across the whole Catholic world, whether the Church is at a solemn Mass or in a
little house at the end of the day.
