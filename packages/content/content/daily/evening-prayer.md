## The Evening Prayer

Say it after the day's work is done, before the household has settled down.
The hour is not important; the quietness is.

## The Examination of Conscience

Retire a little, and ask yourself these questions honestly. Do not dwell on
what is already forgiven; simply name what happened, and leave it with God.

- Did I do today what I know to be right, and did I leave undone what I know I should have done?
- Did I seek what was just and honest in my dealings with others, or did I deceive anyone by word, by silence, or by keeping what was not mine?
- Did I injure anyone by tongue, by temper, by contempt, or by neglect?
- Did I spend the day in vanity, in idleness, in excess of food or drink, or in anything that offended God and my own soul?
- Did I neglect my prayers, my duties of state, or the care of those entrusted to me?
- Did I fail in charity towards my neighbour, whether by thought, by word, or by deed?

## The Act of Contrition

> O my Lord and my God, I am sorry that I have offended You, because I know that
> You are good and that You alone are deserving of all love. I am sorry because
> I dread the loss of Heaven and the pains of Hell, but most of all because I
> have offended You, my good and sovereign Lord. I firmly resolve, with the help
> of Your grace, to amend my ways and to sin no more. Amen.

## The Act of Faith

> Jesus, I believe in You, for You are the Son of God, the true and eternal God.
> I believe all that Your holy Church teaches. Let this faith increase in me.
> Amen.

## The Petition for Forgiveness

> O Lord Jesus Christ, Who have power to forgive sins, look upon Your Church
> and upon me. Wash me in the Blood of Your Sacred Heart, and make me pure in
> the Blood of Your most precious Blood. Let the Blood and Water flowing from
> Your sacred Heart cleanse my soul, and let the Precious Blood of Jesus Christ
> be the safeguard of my soul. Save me, O Lord, from every evil, and grant me
> the grace to follow Your holy will all the days of my life. Amen.

## The Blessing for the Night

> God of all good gifts, who have kept me safe through the day, now I lay me
> down in Your care. Bless this house, and all who sleep in it. Give them rest
> and peace, and wake them in Your grace. Let no evil come near them, and let
> the Blessed Virgin Mary, St. Joseph, and all the saints of God watch over
> them while they sleep. And keep me, O Lord, in Your holy keeping, and let my
> last night on earth be the night before Your Kingdom. Through Jesus Christ our
> Lord. Amen.

## The Last Prayers

Say the **Our Father**, the **Hail Mary**, and the **Glory Be**, and then the
traditional blessing:

> May the Blessed Virgin Mary, the Mother of God, pray for us together with St.
> Joseph, St. Anthony of Padua, and all the saints, and may their prayers be
> for us with Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

Then make the **Sign of the Cross** and go to rest.
