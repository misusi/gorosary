## The Prayer

It is said before every task of the day, before study, before work, before a
journey, before anything that matters.

> O Jesus, I love Thee. I love Thee with all my heart, with all my soul, with
> all my strength. O sweetest Jesus, teach me to know You, teach me to love
> You, teach me to serve You, and make me worthy to behold You face to face in
> the kingdom of heaven. Amen.

## The Same Prayer Addressed to the Holy Spirit

> O Holy Spirit, I love Thee. I love Thee with all my heart, with all my soul,
> with all my strength. O sweetest Spirit, teach me to know Thee, teach me to
> love Thee, teach me to serve Thee, and make me worthy to behold Thee face to
> face in the kingdom of heaven. Amen.

A traditional short invocation of the Holy Spirit is this:

> Come, Holy Spirit, fill my heart with Your grace, and make my heart a temple
> of Your presence. You are the God of my life and the source of all good.
> Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

## A Note

The custom is older than it is usually credited. In the religious houses of
the older Church, a short petition for the grace of the Holy Spirit was said
before every piece of work — the copying of a page, the digging of a garden,
the mending of a wall. The point is not that the task is holy, but that the
worker may be. Nothing that is done for God is small, and nothing that is
offered to God is done by ourselves alone.
