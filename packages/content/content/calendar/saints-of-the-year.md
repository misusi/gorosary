## The Calendar of Fixed Days

Most of the commemorations below keep the same date every year, so that a
saint can be found on the same day in every country and every century. The
great movable days of the Church, which follow Easter, are listed in
**Feasts and Fasts**.

The dates are those of the General Roman Calendar, the calendar of the whole
Church. National and diocesan calendars add more, and in a few places they
differ; where they do, the local calendar is followed.

Some of the saints here are not commemorations of the General Roman Calendar
but are widely kept in particular countries or religious families. Where that
is so, the note says so, and the kind is given as a commemoration rather than
a memorial.

To find any of these saints in the app, use the search box. To see who is
kept on a particular day, open the Saint of the Day.
